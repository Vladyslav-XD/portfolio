Frequently asked questions

How do I choose what to calculate?

Each sport has three linked numbers: pace (speed on the bike), distance and time. Tap "Calculate" on the card you want as the result: it fills with the sport's colour and shows "= Result". Change the other two numbers and the result updates as you go.

How do I type a number?

Tap the number and use the keypad. Time and pace are typed like a stopwatch: 4, 3, 0 gives 4:30, and the "00" key adds two zeros. Distance and bike speed get a decimal key instead. Tap "Done" when you finish.

Can I change a number without the keypad?

Yes. Drag the number left or right to change it step by step (second by second for time and pace), with a light haptic tick. Or tap one of the quick chips under it.

How do I switch to miles or yards?

Use the units toggle at the top of the screen: KM / MI, or M / YD on Swim.

How does the triathlon tab work?

The Σ tab adds up swim, bike, run and both transitions (T1 and T2) into one total time. Start from a preset (Sprint, Olympic, Half or Full) and adjust each leg.

How do I turn off the jokes in the black band?

Open Settings and switch off "Ticker jokes". The band is also hidden when Reduce Motion is on in your iPhone's accessibility settings.

Can I see the tips again?

Yes: Settings › Onboarding › Show again.

How do I change the language or theme?

In Settings. Language: System language, English or Українська. Theme: System, Light or Dark.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. The app works offline, without sign-up, and there are no ads.

Is the app free?

Yes, completely.
