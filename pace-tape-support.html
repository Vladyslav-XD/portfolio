PACE TAPE · SUPPORT

Support

iOS app · version 1.2.1 · November 2026

Thanks for using Pace Tape. If something isn’t working or you have an idea, write to filon.design.ux@gmail.com and I’ll reply, usually within a few days. I read every message. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version. A screenshot helps a lot.

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose what to calculate?

Each sport has three linked numbers: pace (speed on the bike), distance and time. One of them is the result. Tap “Calculate” on the card you want as the result: it fills with the sport’s colour and shows “= Result”. Change the other two and the result follows.

How do I type a number?

Tap the number and use the keypad. Time and pace are typed like a stopwatch, digits only: 4 3 0 gives 4:30, and the “00” key adds two zeros. Where a number can have a decimal part, such as kilometres or bike speed, that key becomes a decimal key. To finish, tap “Done”, tap outside the keypad or swipe it down. What you typed is kept.

Can I change a number without the keypad?

Yes, three ways. Drag the number left or right to change it step by step (second by second for time and pace). Tap ‹ or › next to it for one step, or hold to keep going. Or tap one of the quick values under it.

How do I switch to miles or yards?

Use the switch at the top of the screen: kilometres or miles, metres or yards on Swim. The values convert.

How does the triathlon tab work?

The Triathlon tab (Σ) adds up swim, bike, run and both transitions (T1 and T2) into one finish time. Start from a distance (Sprint, Olympic, Half or Full) and set the pace of each leg. You can also tap the finish time and type the one you want. The legs without a lock change their pace to fit it. A lock keeps a leg as it is.

How do I turn off the ticker and the animations?

Open More (…) → Settings and switch on “Reduce Motion”. If Reduce Motion is on in iPhone Settings, the app follows it too.

Can I see the tips again?

Yes. Open More (…) → How the app works. Each tip there has “Show on screen ›”.

How do I change the language or theme?

In More (…) → Settings. Language: System language, English, Українська, Deutsch or Français. Theme: System, Light or Dark.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. The app works offline and without sign-up, and it has no ads.

Is the app free?

Yes. Everything in the app is free. If you want to thank me, open More (…) → Aid station: a gel, a bottle or a pasta party. It’s a one-off App Store payment, not a subscription, and it unlocks nothing.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone with iOS 15 or later.

Privacy

Your numbers never leave your phone. Versions 1.0 and 1.1 send nothing. From version 1.2 the app sends usage stats only after you tap Yes on the card it shows after the launch screen. The stats say which screens and features are used, never your numbers or your name. To turn them off, open More (…) → Settings → Usage stats. Details: Privacy Policy
