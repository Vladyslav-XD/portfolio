PACE TAPE · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026 (app version 1.2)

Pace Tape is an iOS app by Vladyslav Filon: a pace, speed and time calculator for swimming, cycling, running and triathlon. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your numbers never leave your phone. From version 1.2 the app can send usage stats, but only if you say yes, and you can change your mind at any time in Settings.

What the app stores on your device

So that it opens the way you left it, the app saves your settings (theme, language, units, haptics, Reduce Motion and your answer about usage stats), the last sport you used together with the numbers you entered, which tips you have already seen, and whether you have left a tip in the Aid station. If you said yes to usage stats, it also keeps the random number described below and any statistics not yet sent. All of this is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Apart from the usage stats you agreed to, none of it is sent anywhere. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. It may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer backups) according to your own iOS settings.

Calculations stay on your device

Every calculation happens on your device, and the app works without an internet connection. The numbers you type or the app works out (pace, speed, distance, times, finish time, incline, transitions) never leave your phone.

Usage stats (version 1.2 and later)

Versions 1.0 and 1.1 send nothing. From version 1.2 the app asks you, on a card after the launch screen, whether you want to share usage stats. Until you tap Yes, nothing is stored and nothing is sent for statistics; if you tap No, it stays that way. The stats show which screens and features are used, so that we can see which parts of the app need a clearer design.

The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Storing the random number on your phone also needs your consent under the ePrivacy Directive, Article 5(3), which Ireland implements in S.I. No. 336 of 2011.

What is sent after you tap Yes:

  • Names of actions: which screens and features you use, when the app is installed or updated, opened or moved to the background, and, if you leave a tip in the Aid station, which tip you chose. Never the price or any payment details.
  • Your app settings with each action: theme, units, language, haptics and Reduce Motion (the app's switch and the iPhone setting).
  • What the analytics library adds to every event: the app version and build and whether it is a TestFlight build, the device model (such as “iPhone17,1”), the iOS version, the language and region setting, the time zone, the screen size, and whether the phone is on Wi-Fi or mobile data (not the carrier's name).
  • A random number that the app creates on your phone after you tap Yes, to tell visits apart, and a session number that changes each time you use the app. The random number is not your name, phone number or Apple ID, not Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) or vendor identifier (IDFV), and it doesn't say who you are. Because of this number, the data is pseudonymous.

What is never sent:

  • Any number you type or the app calculates.
  • Your name, email address, location, contacts, Apple ID, advertising identifier (IDFA) or vendor identifier (IDFV).

The stats go to PostHog, an analytics service that processes them on our behalf in PostHog EU Cloud, on servers in the European Union. The project is set to discard IP addresses and not to work out a location from them, so neither your IP address nor your location is stored, and no profile is built for you. Nothing is used to track you across other apps or websites, nothing is sold and nothing is used for ads. The stats are kept for up to one year and then deleted.

You can withdraw your consent at any time: More (…) → Settings → Usage stats, switch off. Sending stops at once, and the random number and any statistics not yet sent are deleted from your phone. Nothing is sent about the switch-off itself. What was sent before can't be linked to you, so we can't find or delete it on request (GDPR Article 11); it is deleted when its year runs out. If you switch usage stats on again, the app creates a new random number.

Purchases

The app is free. If you choose to leave a tip in the Aid station, the purchase is made through the App Store under Apple's terms and privacy policy. We never receive your name, card or any other payment details; the app only remembers on your device that you have tipped.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No ads, no advertising SDKs, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No access to your location, health data, contacts, camera or photos.
  • No collection of names, email addresses, location or contacts.
  • No data sold or shared, apart from PostHog processing usage stats on our behalf.

Apple

Downloads are handled by the App Store under Apple's own privacy policy. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide aggregated statistics and crash reports that don't identify you. They are used only to fix bugs.

Your rights

You can withdraw your consent to usage stats at any time in Settings, as described above. Because the stats can't be linked to you, we can't find, export or delete one person's records on request (GDPR Article 11). Questions and requests are welcome at the address below. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (dataprotection.ie) or to the data protection authority in your own country.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Apart from the usage stats described above, which are sent only if you say yes and can't be linked to you, it collects no information from anyone. If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before tapping Yes.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date changed.

24 September 2026: first version, nothing collected. 29 October 2026: version 1.2, usage stats only with your consent.
