MOCKTAIL FINDER · SUPPORT

Support

iOS app · version 1.2

Thanks for using Mocktail Finder. If something isn't working or you have an idea, write to filon.design.ux@gmail.com — I read every message and usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. The app works without sign-up, and there are no ads.

Does the app need the internet?

No. All recipes, photos and collections are built in, so everything works offline. The only thing that needs a connection is buying or restoring a purchase, which goes through the App Store.

Where are my favourites, my bar and my own recipes stored?

On your device only. They survive app updates, but they are removed if you delete the app. They are not synced between devices yet.

What is Mocktail Finder Pro?

A one-time purchase — no subscription, no account — that adds My Bar (tick what's at home and see what you can mix right now), a shopping list, servings from 1 to 12 that scale every measure, and recipe cards to share as a photo. Everything that was free stays free.

What are the collections and "Everything"?

A collection is a set of ten tested 0.0% drinks with their own photos, sold separately as a one-time purchase. "Everything" is Pro plus all collections, including the ones added later, in one purchase.

I reinstalled the app or got a new iPhone. How do I get my purchases back?

Open About (the "…" button in the header) and tap "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so sign in with the same one. Restore is also on the Pro screen and on a collection's sheet.

A purchase failed or says "waiting for approval"

If a purchase fails, nothing is charged — just try again. "Waiting for approval" means Ask to Buy is on for your Apple ID: the purchase unlocks as soon as the family organiser approves it. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, because payments go through the App Store, not through us.

What do the tips do?

Nothing — they unlock no features. They are simply a way to say thanks, and the app says so right under the cards.

Can I add a photo to my own recipe?

Yes. Tap "Add Photo" in the Add Recipe form, then choose "Take Photo" or "Choose from Library". The photo is stored on your device only. If you declined camera access, you can turn it on in Settings › Mocktail Finder.

How do I edit or delete my own recipe?

Open the recipe and scroll to the bottom: "Edit" opens it in the form again, "Delete" removes it together with its photo after a confirmation. Both are available only for recipes you created yourself.

How do I change the language or the theme?

Open About (the "…" button in the header). Language: System, English or Ukrainian. Theme: System, Light or Dark. The app remembers your choice.

How do I see the tour again?

Open About and tap "Bartender school".

What do the words under each drink mean?

They describe the drink's character — for example "Iced · Citrus · Sparkling" or "Hot · Chocolate · Creamy". They are worked out from the recipe's ingredients and instructions, and the same words are used as filters on the home screen.

How do I share a recipe?

Open any recipe and tap "Share" to send it as text with ingredients and steps, or "Share as card" (Pro) to send it as a photo card.

Is the app free?

Yes. Everything from version 1.1 is free and stays free. Pro, the collections and the tips are optional one-time purchases.
