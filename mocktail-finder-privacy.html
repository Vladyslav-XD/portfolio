MOCKTAIL FINDER · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Mocktail Finder is an iOS app by Vladyslav Filon: your alcohol-free home bar, with recipes, your own bar, a shopping list and recipe cards. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the app does not collect any personal data, and it does not talk to any server of ours.

What the app stores on your device

Your favourite recipes, the recipes you create yourself, the ingredients you tick in My Bar, your shopping list, your language and theme choices and the state of your purchases are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. If you attach a photo to your own recipe, a copy of that photo is stored in the same place. None of this is uploaded anywhere and none of it is ever seen by us. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. It may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer backups) according to your own iOS settings.

Works offline

All recipes, photos and collections are built into the app. The app makes no internet requests of its own: it does not contact any server of ours and it does not use any third-party recipe or image service. The only network activity is the App Store's own, when you buy or restore a purchase. This applies to version 1.2 and later; versions 1.0 and 1.1 loaded the free catalogue from TheCocktailDB over the internet, so if you still use one of them, please update.

Purchases

Mocktail Finder Pro, the recipe collections and the tips are one-time in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details, your Apple ID or your name. The app stores on your device only whether a purchase is active, so it can unlock what you bought. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and can be restored on another device with "Restore Purchases". Tips unlock nothing — they are a thank-you.

Photos

When you add a photo to your own recipe, you can take one with the camera or pick one from your library. The camera is used only at that moment, to take that one picture; the app never records video or audio and does not use the camera for anything else. iOS asks for your permission the first time, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings — the library option keeps working without it. Picking from the library uses the standard iOS photo picker, so the app receives only the photo you pick, not access to your whole library. Either way the photo is stored on your device only and is never uploaded. If you add no photo, the app shows a built-in placeholder image.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • No servers and no internet requests of our own.
  • No collection of names, email addresses, contacts, location, photos or any other personal data.
  • No data sold or shared with anyone.

Sharing recipes

When you use "Share" or "Share as card", the app hands the recipe text or the recipe card image to the iOS share sheet. What happens next is controlled by the app you choose to share with (Messages, Mail and so on) and by your own choices.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date changed. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
